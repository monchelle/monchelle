The Purpose of This Plan

To drive business uplift in the Baltimore area, we are establishing a peer support structure based on enablement, guidance, and strategic backup. Rather than taking over daily tasks, this plan focuses on helping peer leaders build more effective and efficient ways of working, providing professional coaching advice, and offering reliable operational relief during peak projects or planned PTO.

By working Tillsammans (Together), we can align performance standards across Kitchen Planning and Kitchen Phones, share best practices, and build a more resilient frontline leadership tier.

The 3-Phase Peer Alignment Roadmap

A structured approach to uncovering individual challenges, aligning standards, and delivering strategic support.

1
Phase 1: Discover & Listen

Identify Challenges & Strengths

Conduct friendly 1-on-1 virtual chats ("Fika") to uncover what each leader defines as their greatest strengths and operational challenges. Build a safe space for collaborative problem-solving.

  • Listen to current pain points & software friction
  • Map out team-specific strengths and expertise
  • Identify areas for shared ways of working
Philosophy: Mutual Respect & Simplicity
2
Phase 2: Coach & Align

Build Guidance Toolkits

Formulate standardized guides for effective remote management. Introduce calibrated coaching methodologies, template-driven team meetings, and streamlined workflows.

  • Design meeting templates & QA checklists
  • Align on corrective action guidelines
  • Co-develop efficient administrative trackers
Philosophy: Lead by Example
3
Phase 3: Support & Cover

Contingent Backup & Advisory

Stand ready as a peer advisor for complex HR situations, co-facilitating calibrations, and stepping in to handle critical payroll/PTO coverage during peer vacations or heavy projects.

  • Act as a sounding board for tough coaching calls
  • Provide admin coverage during planned PTO
  • Absorb extra load during peak business projects
Philosophy: Togetherness & Trust

Governance & Alignment (Supporting the Remote Area Manager)

To keep our Remote Area Manager fully informed and aligned, we will establish a structured, lightweight feedback loop. This guarantees full visibility into our achievements, co-worker performance levels, and operational bottlenecks without adding unnecessary overhead.

Weekly Briefing

A quick, high-impact update to the RAM on shared best practices introduced, efficiency metrics, and upcoming PTO/Project backup schedules.

Bi-Weekly Peer Roundtable

A collaborative 30-minute roundtable check-in with the Baltimore TLs to share coaching wins, calibrate QA, and review upcoming PTO/projects needing coverage support.

Monthly Business Review

A joint alignment call with the Remote Area Manager and peers to analyze performance indicators (NPS, conversion, payroll SLA compliance, team attendance).
