# 📊 CSAT Forum Analysis & Improvement Project

This repository contains the **anonymized presentation and supporting analysis** for the **Happy Customer Score (HCS) Forum**, part of the FY27 improvement initiative.  
The project demonstrates applied data analytics, sentiment modeling, and leadership insights developed to improve customer experience performance across the Retail Contact Center.

---

## 🎯 Project Overview

The **HCS Forum Analysis** explores how customer sentiment and quality metrics correlate with performance outcomes.  
It identifies coaching opportunities, trust‑building behaviors, and process barriers that influence customer satisfaction.

This version has been **fully anonymized** for portfolio publication:
- All brand references replaced with *Retail Contact Center* terminology  
- All coworker names replaced with *Agent* and *Leader* identifiers  
- Proprietary visuals and internal logos removed  

---

## 🧠 Analytical Focus

The analysis integrates multiple data perspectives:

| Dimension | Description |
|------------|--------------|
| **Performance Distribution** | Quartile analysis by employee and supervisor from Week 18–36 (May–Aug) |
| **Sentiment Modeling** | Correlation between Knowledge, Manner, and Trust attributes and HCS outcomes |
| **High‑Risk vs. High‑Performing Cohorts** | Identification of agents with recurring low or high sentiment patterns |
| **Queue‑Level Insights** | Comparative analysis of functional areas to isolate process‑driven risks |
| **Quality Correlation** | Relationship between quality evaluations and customer sentiment |
| **Recommended Actions** | Coaching, trust‑building, and leadership accountability strategies |

---

## 🔍 Key Findings

- **Trust** is the strongest predictor of customer satisfaction (r ≈ 0.86).  
- **Quality** correlates moderately with HCS (r ≈ 0.32 overall, strengthening with larger samples).  
- **Survey volume** is not a reliable performance indicator but improves confidence in results.  
- **Sustained coaching** and **leadership consistency** are required to close performance gaps.  
- Reaching the FY27 goal of **86 HCS** aligns with maintaining a **96.64% average quality score**.
